About us

Built around the reading needs of our community

Student's Union Library & Recreation Centre serves readers of different age groups in and around Poonthura. Our goal is to make books, learning resources and community activities easier to access.

This website is designed as the public-facing home of the library and can later be connected to the library's online management software.
